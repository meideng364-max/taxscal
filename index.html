This calculator helps you work out your take-home pay. Enter your basic salary and any benefits in Kenyan shillings, then click Submit. The calculator adds the two together to get your gross salary, then works out the statutory deductions: NHIF for health insurance, NSSF for your pension contribution, and NHDF for the housing levy. Each amount appears in the results table below. These figures are estimates, so confirm the current rates with KRA or your employer.
